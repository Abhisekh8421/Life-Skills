# Learning Process Reflection

## Question 1  
### What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a learning process where I learn a concept by explaining it in simple terms as if I am teaching someone else.

---

## Question 2  
### Most interesting idea from the video *Learning How to Learn*

The most interesting idea that I got from the video is that the brain has two different modes of thinking, and both are needed for learning. I liked the video’s explanation that breaks the myth that taking a break is a waste of time and actually helps the brain connect ideas better.

---

## Question 3  
### What are Active and Diffused Modes of Thinking?

- **Active (Focused) Mode**:  
  This is when I am actively concentrating on a problem, like when I am solving coding problems or learning a concept step by step.

- **Diffused Mode**:  
  This is when my brain is in a relaxed state, like when I am walking or resting, and it helps me see the bigger picture and connect ideas naturally.

## Question 4  
### Steps to approach a new topic

- Decide exactly what I want to learn  
- Break the topic into small sub-parts  
- Remove distractions and focus properly  
- Practice the skill as much as possible  
- Get quick feedback and fix mistakes
