# Learning Process Reflection

## Question 1  
### What is the Feynman Technique?

Feynman Technique: A technique to understand something by explaining it in simple words as if you were explaining it to someone else.

## Question 3  
### What are Active and Diffused Modes of Thinking?

- Active (Focused) Mode:
  This is when I am fully concentrating on a problem, like solving coding problems or learning a concept step by step.

- Diffused Mode:
  This is when my brain is in a relaxed state, like when I am walking or resting, and helps me get the bigger picture and naturally link ideas.

## Question 4  
### Steps to approach a new topic

- Decide exactly what I want to learn
- Break the topic into small parts
- Remove distractions and focus properly
- Practice the skill as much as I can
- Get quick feedback and fix mistakes
