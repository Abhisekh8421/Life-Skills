# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

Grit means having passion and patience to work on one goal for a long time without giving up. Success does not come from talent alone; it comes from continuous effort and a never-quit attitude.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

A growth mindset means believing that our skills and intelligence can improve with practice and effort. If we work hard and learn from mistakes, we can get better at anything.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

**What is Internal Locus of Control?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our success or failure mainly depends on our own actions, not on luck or other people.

We should focus on what we can control, like our effort, attitude, and discipline. If we blame others or luck, we lose motivation. But if we take responsibility, we grow stronger.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

**Key Points mentioned by the speaker:**

* Accept challenges
* Learn from mistakes
* Stop saying “I can’t”
* Add “yet” after failures
* Take feedback positively
* Focus on learning, not just results
* Practice regularly
* Stay consistent

## 5. Mindset – A MountBlue Warrior Reference

### Question 5

**My ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset (as a Software Developer):**

* Practice coding daily, even if it's a small problem
* Do not fear errors; debug and learn from them
* Ask questions without hesitation
* Take feedback from seniors positively
* Work on weak areas instead of avoiding them
* Stay consistent in learning DSA and development
* Focus on improving 1% daily
* Do not compare with others; compare with my past self
* Take ownership of my mistakes
* Keep long-term goals in mind and work patiently

**Final Thought:**
Talent is good, but hard work, consistency, and the right mindset are more important. If we believe we can improve and keep working, success will come slowly but surely.
