# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment is any behavior which makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected. It is normally verbal, visual, or physical.


### Examples include:

- Making dirty jokes or sexual remarks
- Sending inappropriate messages, photos, or videos
- Inappropriate touching
- Staring at someone in a way that makes you uncomfortable

---

## 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

### If I face sexual harassment:

- I will ask them to stop.
- I will keep all the messages, emails, and/or photographs as evidence.
- I will find a witness.
- I will report to the HR or ICC.


### If I see someone being harassed:

- I will ask them to report the harassment.
- In case of severe harassment, I will report the incident.
