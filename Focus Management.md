# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep work is the process of committing yourself entirely to one task without any distractions.

It also includes avoiding shallow tasks such as reading your emails or browsing your phone. This concentration saves a lot of time too.
 
According to Cal Newport, this kind of focus is not common in today’s world, and it will make you more valuable in your profession.

---

## Question 2: How to do deep work properly?

### 1. Take breaks

Avoid trying to concentrate all day. After 60–90 minutes of intense work, take a serious break.

### 2. Avoiding interruptions

Move your phone to a different room. Instruct others not to bother you.

### 3. Establish a schedule

Do deep work every day at the same time. Your brain adjusts to it.

### 4. Put an end to checking the phone.

When working deeply, avoid checking your email or messages. You become distracted each time you switch.

---

## Question 3: How can I use this in my daily life?

Allocate a time slot for deep work from 10 AM to 1 PM and protect it from distractions.

Keep my phone out of reach, either in my bag or turned off.

Ease into it: If an hour is too long, break it into 25-minute chunks and stretch it out.

Draw up a plan for a short break, such as:

- Take a walk
- Meet friends or family
- Listen to some songs
- Watch a few interesting podcasts

## Question 4: What are the dangers of social media?

### 1. Kills focus

Social media apps grab attention. Using them makes it harder to focus on work.

### 2. Makes you compare

Seeing everyone's happy moments makes you feel your life is boring.

### 3. Wastes time

You open your phone for 2 minutes and end up scrolling for 40 minutes.

### 4. Reduces attention span

These days short-form content reduces the attention span.
